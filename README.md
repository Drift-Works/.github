## What we're building

### Halos · flagship
**The intelligence and reporting layer above your security tools.**

Halos is a portable infrastructure-intelligence appliance. Dropped into an unfamiliar
network, it observes the environment, ingests what's already there (SIEM, EDR, Security
Onion), correlates it, and tells you what exists, what's exposed, what controls are missing,
and what to fix first. It is the parachute for anyone handed a network they didn't build.

Halos isn't a SIEM, an EDR, or a scanner. It sits above those and makes sense of them, for
the under-resourced operator who can't afford the enterprise stack: the fractional CISO, the
CTO who inherited a mess, the team of one.

Currently in active build. Teaser at [driftworks.nyc/halos](https://driftworks.nyc/halos).

### Sonder · active
An autonomous DJ engine that reads a room. Sonder listens to the stories, memories, and
sentiments people share, then translates that human context into sound. A quieter project,
worked in the spaces between, and very much alive.

## How we build

DriftWorks runs as a founder plus an AI C-suite: six officers (COO, CTO, CISO, CPO, CFO,
CMO) who own outcomes, not opinions, with Ross Chestnut as CEO and final authority. Every
decision is recorded, every session leaves a trail, and the studio's own operating system is
versioned like a product. If you're evaluating how a lean studio ships serious work, that
trail is the case study.
